Kamon, taken apart

Twenty crests of the Sengoku houses, broken down into the compass strokes they were drawn with. Then a workshop where you can draw your own.

Twenty houses

Every crest here was worn into battle between 1560 and 1600. Re-sort them to see what they share, or pick one to take it apart.

Workshop

Every crest in this workshop is built from the same parts as the real ones: a motif, repeated in an arrangement, inside an enclosure. The name updates as you build, following the traditional naming rules.
